App Support - Common Questions

Photo button closes window?

Fixed in Build 36! Update to latest build from TestFlight. Photo now uses native iPhone camera and will not close detail window. Tap 📷 Photo -> Camera opens -> Take photo -> ✅ Saved.

How to add company logo?

Tap ⚙️ Company -> Logo -> Choose file. Logo stored locally on device.

How to save report?

Fill inspection, tap 💾 SAVE (top bar), then ↗ Preview -> 📧 Email or 🖨️ Print/PDF.

Where is data stored?

All data stored locally on your iPhone/iPad. No cloud, no tracking. See Privacy Policy.

Bathrooms - Toilets, Sinks, etc?

Build 33+ : Each bathroom has separate items: Toilet, Sink, Countertop, Cabinets, Shower/Tub - each with Condition, Photo, Comment.

Conditions dropdown?

Build 32+ : Conditions are dropdown: Appeared to operate normally, Appeared in good condition, Tested faulty, Flawed.

TREC Compliance

App designed for Texas TREC Standards of Practice. Includes I / NI / NP / D reporting, Pre-Inspection Agreement with signature, 18 sections, 170+ items.
