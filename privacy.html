Introduction

UHRI HOME BUYERS INSPECTION SERVICE ("UHRI", "we", "us", "our") operates the UHRI Home Buyers Inspection mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in the App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of your clients.

Data Collection - No Data Collected

The UHRI App does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

  • All inspection data, customer names, property addresses, photos, notes, TREC checklist data (I/NI/NP/D), agreement signatures, and company logo are stored locally on your iPad/iPhone using localStorage.
  • We do not have access to your inspection reports, client information, or photos.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising SDKs, or third-party data collection tools.
  • We do not sell, share, or rent any data.

Local Storage

Data you enter is stored locally on your device:

  • Customer Name, Property Address, Inspection Date
  • Pre-Inspection Agreement signature status
  • Property Description, Exterior, Roof, Structural, Interior Rooms, Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC, Appliances checklist data
  • Company Settings: Company Name, Phone, Email, TREC License, Inspector Name, Address, Website, Company Logo (stored as base64 data URL locally)
  • Photos you add (stored locally, not uploaded to cloud by us)

You can clear this data at any time via iPad Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data, or by deleting the App.

Photos

Photos taken or selected within the App are stored locally on your device and included in your local report. They are not uploaded to our servers. If you use the Limited Share feature, you control where photos are shared (email, etc.).

Company Logo

Company logo you upload is converted to a data URL and stored locally in your browser's localStorage (uhri_company_logo). It never leaves your device except when included in reports you generate and share.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services that collect data. The App is a standalone HTML/JavaScript application running inside a WKWebView.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Texas TREC Compliance

The App is designed to comply with Texas Real Estate Commission (TREC) Standards of Practice for inspection reports, including pre-inspection agreement requirements and I/NI/NP/D reporting.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a new Effective Date.
